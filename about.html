About me / Itqan Codes

Designer by craft. Engineer by mindset. Driven by purpose.

I'm Akinjobi Abdurrahman, a web designer, Mechanical Engineering student, Qur'ān tutor, and technology enthusiast based in Ogun State, Nigeria.

I enjoy working at the intersection of creativity, technology, engineering, and purposeful problem-solving. My approach to design goes beyond making things look beautiful. I believe great digital experiences should be intentional, functional, accessible, and capable of solving real problems.

As a web designer, I create clean, modern, and user-focused websites and digital experiences that help individuals, brands, and businesses communicate their value effectively. I've had the opportunity to work with different companies, gaining experience, learning from different environments, and continuously refining my creative and technical abilities.

The engineering mindset

My journey in Mechanical Engineering has significantly influenced the way I approach life and problem-solving. I'm naturally curious about how things work, why systems behave the way they do, and how complex ideas can be broken down into understandable solutions.

I enjoy research, technical exploration, and learning about mechanical systems. Engines, machines, engineering principles, and the science behind everyday technology particularly fascinate me.

I believe engineering and design share something important: both require creativity, critical thinking, patience, and the ability to transform ideas into practical solutions.

Beyond design and engineering

There's more to me than technology and engineering.

I'm also a Qur'ān tutor, and I consider teaching and sharing beneficial knowledge an important part of my personal development. I value Islamic education, continuous learning, good character, and contributing positively to the lives of others.

I'm particularly interested in initiatives and projects that can create meaningful benefits for the Islamic community. I'm currently exploring and developing several ideas that, bi-idhnillāh, will contribute positively to the community.

What drives me

I'm motivated by curiosity, creativity, knowledge, and the opportunity to build things that matter.

Whether I'm designing a website, studying an engineering concept, researching a new idea, teaching the Qur'ān, or working on a personal project, I approach each responsibility with a willingness to learn and improve.

I value purposeful work over empty activity, meaningful collaboration over unnecessary competition, and continuous improvement over standing still.

My long-term ambition is to keep growing across technology, engineering, entrepreneurship, and community development while using my skills and knowledge to create solutions that make a positive difference.

I'm always open to meaningful collaborations, creative opportunities, interesting projects, and conversations with people who love building, learning, and making an impact.
